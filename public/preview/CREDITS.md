# Bundled preview assets

The active maps, block characters and pixel textures are original procedural assets generated by the editor. No Minecraft textures, skins or models are incorporated. The historical city and downloaded character models below are retained in the source checkout for regression tests and excluded from release packages, together with the city's derived collision data. The following notices document those historical downloads.
